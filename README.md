# Super_Sales-Dashboard-using-tableau

The SuperSales Dashboard is an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive overview of a company’s sales and profit performance. The dashboard includes key performance indicators (KPIs), visual comparisons, trend forecasts, and regional breakdowns, allowing stakeholders to make informed decisions.

Key Features:
  Total Sales: 2,297,201</br>
  Total Profit: 286,397</br>
  Profit Ratio: 0.1247</br>
  Quantity Sold: 9,994</br>
  Number of Customers: 9,994</br>
  Top and Bottom Subcategories by Profit</br>
  Visualizes the most and least profitable product subcategories (e.g., Copiers and Phones as top performers; Tables and Bookcases at the bottom).</br>
  Sales Forecasting Over Years (2014–2018):</br>
  Displays yearly sales trends and forecasts using a line chart, identifying peak and dip years in sales volume.</br>
  Total Profit by Region:</br>
  Donut chart showing profit contributions from four regions: West ($108.42K), East ($91.52K), South ($46.75K), and Central ($39.71K).</br>
  Dynamic Parameterized Chart:</br>
  A bar chart driven by a parameter selector allowing users to toggle between Total Sales, Total Profit, and Quantity to view subcategory performance.</br>
  Subcategory Order Trends:</br>

  Line chart depicting order counts for each subcategory, helping to identify patterns in product returns or order frequency.</br>

Outcome:
This dashboard helps executives, analysts, and sales managers identify high-performing products, track sales growth, and assess regional profitability. It enables data-driven decision-making for inventory planning, marketing strategy, and customer targeting.
