# Super_Sales-Dashboard-using-tableau

The SuperSales Dashboard is an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive overview of a company’s sales and profit performance. The dashboard includes key performance indicators (KPIs), visual comparisons, trend forecasts, and regional breakdowns, allowing stakeholders to make informed decisions.

Key Features:
  Total Sales: 2,297,201
  Total Profit: 286,397
  Profit Ratio: 0.1247
  Quantity Sold: 9,994
  Number of Customers: 9,994
  Top and Bottom Subcategories by Profit:
  Visualizes the most and least profitable product subcategories (e.g., Copiers and Phones as top performers; Tables and Bookcases at the bottom).
  Sales Forecasting Over Years (2014–2018):
  Displays yearly sales trends and forecasts using a line chart, identifying peak and dip years in sales volume.
  Total Profit by Region:
  Donut chart showing profit contributions from four regions: West ($108.42K), East ($91.52K), South ($46.75K), and Central ($39.71K).
  Dynamic Parameterized Chart:
  A bar chart driven by a parameter selector allowing users to toggle between Total Sales, Total Profit, and Quantity to view subcategory performance.
  Subcategory Order Trends:

  Line chart depicting order counts for each subcategory, helping to identify patterns in product returns or order frequency.

Outcome:
This dashboard helps executives, analysts, and sales managers identify high-performing products, track sales growth, and assess regional profitability. It enables data-driven decision-making for inventory planning, marketing strategy, and customer targeting.
